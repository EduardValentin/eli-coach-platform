# Design

## Identity

Evoa has a warm, premium, and modern visual identity. Soft off-white surfaces, restrained borders and shadows, and generous whitespace keep the product calm and approachable. Magenta is the primary brand and action color; teal is the grounded counterpoint for secondary actions and recovery-oriented content. Elegant serif headings in `Playfair Display` add personality, while `DM Sans` keeps the interface practical and easy to scan.

The design balances human coaching with professional competence. Photography and small product-UI compositions may add warmth and context, but the interface itself stays clean, focused, and consistent across the public site, client portal, and coach portal. The landing page carries more visual impact and motion than the portals, which favor calm, predictable structure for use mid-workout and while managing detailed plans.

## Audience

Women seeking personalized strength and nutrition coaching, with or without an active menstrual cycle. The warm palette, editorial headings, supportive copy hierarchy, and body-aware domain colors for training day types, cycle phases, and flow intensity make the experience feel personal without stereotypically feminine decoration.

## Source of Truth

The design system lives in code. Read it there rather than expecting a catalogue here:

- Production tokens: `packages/ui/src/styles.css`. Production components and variants: `packages/ui` has no root barrel; it publishes eleven concern subpaths instead (`appointments`, `calendar`, `filters`, `layout`, `lib`, `motion`, `overlays`, `portal`, `primitives`, `tabs`, `toast`), each imported on its own; `portal` holds the page chrome both portals share (page header, widget frame, widget link, reading, settings frame, empty state).
- The prototype in `designs/react-reference-app` mirrors the same visual roles and may carry tokens and compositions production does not have yet. Its `DESIGN.md` and `brand-voice.md` describe it.

Every color, spacing, radius, shadow, and typography value is a semantic token; raw values appear only in non-reusable layout mechanics. A token name means the same value in production and in the prototype. Radii, control sizes and letter-spacing are named by role (`rounded-card`, `rounded-field`, `rounded-control`, `size-control-lg`, `tracking-label`); the framework's size-named steps are never overridden, and a role that would equal one (0.025em tracking is `tracking-wide`) is not added. Every role name is registered with `cn`'s class merger, so a later class of the same kind replaces it. Every text action is a `Button` or `buttonVariants`: one base whose `corner` is the 8px `field` corner or the 14px `control` one, sizes that set a height, a padding that narrows beside a leading or trailing icon (`md-wide` keeps its padding), and a text size (`xl` is the 60px checkout action; round icon sizes set only a size), widths that set only a width, one raised elevation, one disabled look and the one focus rule; `ghost-muted` is the ghost look in secondary ink at rest, for the quiet shell controls such as the menu toggle and Sign out. The interaction colour is `primary`: the brand magenta on the public site and inside the client portal, near-black inside the coach portal, set by the document's `data-portal` scope so one `primary` button, chip, tab or link reads correctly in every place. Two fills stay outside that scope: `ink`, near-black turning brand on hover, and `on-brand`, the base surface with brand text for an action on a brand-coloured banner. `ink`, the text colour as a fill, is the bundle selector's checkout action, and `inverted`, the inverted surface as a fill, is the action of the error, access, call-join, download and cart surfaces; they are distinct button roles, not synonyms. Every card is a `Card` or `cardVariants`: `card` (16px, faint border, card shadow), `quiet` (flat, on the quiet surface), `panel` (24px, for large frames), `portal-panel` (24px, the faint hairline the coach portal's panels carry) or `inset` (16px, the subtle hairline on a tint of the quiet surface with its own 16px padding, for an agreement or aside set inside a larger frame). A pill of state is a `Badge`, in one of six tones: `brand-secondary` on the secondary-brand surface, `secondary`, the borderless neutral pill on the `secondary` surface in `secondary-foreground` ink, the outlined `muted` one, `pending` and `success` on their status surfaces, and `count` for a number inside a tab or a `Select` option; a page run is the `Pagination` parts; a status filter that owns its panels is the segmented `Tabs` subpath, while a row of deselectable chips stays `FilterChipGroup`; an appointment is the `AppointmentCard` or, in a dashboard panel, the `DashboardAppointmentRow`, and its row actions are `RowActionButton` and `RowActionLink`, the compact icon-and-label controls. There is one checkbox look, and one multi-choice chip look (`CheckboxChip`), a pill that reads as a native checkbox to assistive technology while carrying its own 44px touch target; a checkbox with a visible statement is a `CheckboxField`, set `inline` beside a short option or as a `statement` whose box aligns to its first line, framed `inset` when it is an agreement, with its problem named through `aria-describedby`. Choosing among several options is a `Select`, matching `Input`'s field height, corner and border, whose options gather under a `SelectGroup` headed by a `SelectLabel` in the muted caption and set apart by a hairline `SelectSeparator`, and choosing one of two described options is a `RadioGroup`. Choosing one of a few short options inline, such as a measurement system, is a `ChoiceGroup`: a segmented row of `ChoiceOption`s on a tint of the quiet surface, the checked one filled in `primary`, one radio group to assistive technology. A date entry is a `DateField`, a trigger in `Input`'s field frame that spells the chosen day out and opens the `Calendar` in a `Popover`, with month and year selects when the field spans years. A run of steps is the `Stepper`: the "Step n of m" count with a status slot beside it, above one bar per step that assistive technology does not read. A field's inline problem is a `FieldError`, the danger-toned line the field names through `aria-describedby`, and its description is a `FieldHint`, the muted line named the same way; a `Label` turns danger-toned while its field is in error, a group of fields is headed by a `Legend` in the label ink, a block of fields that is not a fieldset is titled by a `FieldCaption` in the same caption and ink, and a quieter qualifier beside a name, such as a unit or "(optional)", is a `LabelSuffix`. Confirming an action is a `ConfirmDialog`, the one confirmation role, over the `overlay-modal` scrim; a dialog that is not a confirmation is a `Dialog` with `DialogContent`: `compact` is the ConfirmDialog frame, and `wide` has a fixed header, a scrolling body and a footer set off by a rule; a labelled value in a portal widget, such as a profile or subscription fact, is a `Reading`: the tracked uppercase label above a medium value, as a term and definition inside a description list or as two paragraphs, with a large value rung that carries its unit; tabular data is the `Table` parts, and a column that sorts takes `sort` on its `TableHead`, which renders `aria-sort` and a sort button; disclosure sections are the `Accordion` parts; a success mark sits on the `success-surface` token. A save outcome is a toast: one `Toaster` per shell, and `toast()` the one way a component reports one. Controls inside `data-surface="inverted"` — the hero, and the header while it is transparent over it — take the lighter focus ring. Weights are regular, medium and semibold, with light kept for the hero's lead copy over video and bold for prices and tracked uppercase `micro` labels. The product targets WCAG AA, keyboard operability everywhere, and layout-stable reduced-motion fallbacks.

Both portals share one `PortalShell`. On large screens a fixed sidebar carries the coach's brand block or, in the client portal, her name block, an icon medallion beside her full name, above the navigation. The medallion is its own role rather than an `Avatar`: a round `User` glyph in `primary` ink on `primary-soft`, 40px in the sidebar and the More sheet and 36px in the top bar, standing in until a client picture exists. Below `lg` a 64px top bar, named for its portal, carries the same block; the coach's navigation opens in a drawer, while the client has a bottom tab bar of at most four tabs plus More, whose `BottomSheet` holds her name block, the links the bar has no room for and Sign out. The top bar pads its top, and the tab bar and the sheet their bottom, by the device's safe-area insets, so the layout keeps clear of the notch and the home indicator. The skip link is the one control that must paint above every fixed piece of page chrome, the portal sidebar and top bar and the public header included, while a modal dialog or sheet keeps it from taking focus at all, and its layer is set once in the shared skip-link rule. The client portal installs as its own app: its name and short name are "Evoa", its theme colour is `surface-base`, so the title bar reads as part of the shell, and its background colour is `surface-page`; the PWA surface definition holds them as those two tokens' hex values.
