## Cohesion groups

Sets of components expected to change together for one kind of reason. A change whose touched
components all sit in one group is expected; one that spreads across groups is a finding unless
accepted below. A component may belong to more than one group. Proposed by an audit from change
history; confirmed by a person.

| Group | Components | Kind of change it absorbs |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical feature slice | C1 packages/domain, C3 packages/config, C5 packages/ui, C6 packages/infrastructure, one or more feature components (C7 features/store, C8 features/waitlist, C9 features/accounts, C17 features/assessment-calls, C18 features/coaching-sales, C19 features/client-onboarding, C20 features/client-profile, C21 features/client-resources, C22 features/check-ins, C23 features/coach-schedule), the surfaces that register them (C11 surfaces/public-site, C12 surfaces/client-portal, C13 surfaces/coach-portal), C14 apps/platform/src/server, C15 app root (the route registry), the migration journal `apps/platform/db`, C4 packages/content when a slice's copy must read the same in several features, and C16 packages/test-support when the slice's tests need a shared fixture | one user-visible capability delivered end to end, the repository's ticket-cutting unit. A slice usually but not always touches the composition root and the journal, so a member's absence does not on its own make a change uncontained. A slice touches more than one feature component when its feature consumes another's composition handles (a narrow port the producer's composition returns under `handles`) or its tables (foreign keys). Confirmed by the owner |
| Waiting-list mode | C1 packages/domain, C3 packages/config, C6 packages/infrastructure, C8 features/waitlist, C14 apps/platform/src/server, C15 app root, C17 features/assessment-calls, C18 features/coaching-sales, `apps/platform/db` | how waiting-list mode is stored, read and switched: the `WAITLIST_MODE` flag's domain reader, its Postgres repository, the waitlist, booking-window and sales-window consumers, the container that hands one reader to all of them, and the migration that seeds it. The spread across C1, C3, C8, C14 and C15 is owner-accepted; C6, C17 and C18 are added from the code, unconfirmed |
| Coach scheduling | C1 packages/domain, C23 features/coach-schedule, C17 features/assessment-calls, C22 features/check-ins, C14 apps/platform/src/server (the container hands C23's handles on), `apps/platform/db` | the coach's own time and room: slot policy, reservation and overlap rules, the coach's window and meeting room, their tables and constraints. Proposed from change history, unconfirmed |
| Adapter-facing contracts | C6 packages/infrastructure, C7 features/store, C8 features/waitlist, C17 features/assessment-calls, C18 features/coaching-sales, C19 features/client-onboarding, C22 features/check-ins, C14 apps/platform/src/server | a change to the email, bot-detection, management-auth or payments contract, which lands on the C6 contract and adapter and on every feature and platform module that sends, verifies or handles through it at once.
