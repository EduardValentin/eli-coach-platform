# Design

## Identity

Evoa has a warm, premium, and modern visual identity. Soft off-white surfaces, restrained borders and shadows, and generous whitespace keep the product calm and approachable. Magenta is the primary brand and action color; teal is the grounded counterpoint for secondary actions and recovery-oriented content. Elegant serif headings in `Playfair Display` add personality, while `DM Sans` keeps the interface practical and easy to scan.

The design balances human coaching with professional competence. Photography and small product-UI compositions may add warmth and context, but the interface itself stays clean, focused, and consistent across the public site, client portal, and coach portal. The landing page carries more visual impact and motion than the portals, which favor calm, predictable structure for use mid-workout and while managing detailed plans.

## Audience

Women seeking personalized strength and nutrition coaching, with or without an active menstrual cycle. The warm palette, editorial headings, supportive copy hierarchy, and body-aware domain colors for training day types, cycle phases, and flow intensity make the experience feel personal without stereotypically feminine decoration.

## Source of Truth

The design system lives in code. Read it there rather than expecting a catalogue here:

- Production tokens: `packages/ui/src/styles.css`. Production components and variants: `packages/ui` has no root barrel; it publishes eleven concern subpaths instead (`appointments`, `calendar`, `filters`, `layout`, `lib`, `motion`, `overlays`, `portal`, `primitives`, `tabs`, `toast`), each imported on its own; `portal` holds the page chrome both portals share (page header, back link, widget frame, collapsible widget frame, widget link, reading, settings frame, empty state), and `layout` holds the shells and the sheets that frame a page (app shell, portal shell, phone frame, dead-end page, navigation dialog, bottom sheet, responsive sheet dialog and its header, body and actions parts).
- The prototype in `designs/react-reference-app` mirrors the same visual roles and may carry tokens and compositions production does not have yet. Its `DESIGN.md` and `brand-voice.md` describe it.

Every color, spacing, radius, shadow, and typography value is a semantic token; raw values appear only in non-reusable layout mechanics. A token name means the same value in production and in the prototype. Radii, control sizes and letter-spacing are named by role (`rounded-card`, `rounded-field`, `rounded-control`, `size-control-lg`, `tracking-label`); the framework's size-named steps are never overridden, and a role that would equal one (0.025em tracking is `tracking-wide`) is not added. Every role name is registered with `cn`'s class merger, so a later class of the same kind replaces it. Every text action is a `Button` or `buttonVariants`: one base whose `corner` is the 8px `field` corner or the 14px `control` one, sizes that set a height, a padding that narrows beside a leading or trailing icon (`md-wide` keeps its padding), and a text size (`xl` is the 60px checkout action; round icon sizes set a size and their glyph's default, 16px in `icon-xs` and `icon-sm` and 20px in `icon-md`, while a text button's glyph is sized by its caller), widths that set only a width, one raised elevation, one disabled look and the one focus rule; `ghost-muted` is the ghost look in secondary ink at rest, for the quiet shell controls such as the menu toggle and Sign out, and `ghost-ink` is the ghost look that fills with the `ink` pairing on hover, for an icon action a table row reveals. The interaction colour is `primary`: the brand magenta on the public site and inside the client portal, near-black inside the coach portal, set by the document's `data-portal` scope so one `primary` button, chip, tab or link reads correctly in every place. Two fills stay outside that scope: `ink`, near-black turning brand on hover, and `on-brand`, the base surface with brand text for an action on a brand-coloured banner. `ink`, the text colour as a fill, is the bundle selector's checkout action, and `inverted`, the inverted surface as a fill, is the action of the error, access, call-join, download and cart surfaces; they are distinct button roles, not synonyms. Every card is a `Card` or `cardVariants`: `card` (16px, faint border, card shadow), `quiet` (flat, on the quiet surface), `panel` (24px, for large frames), `portal-panel` (24px, the faint hairline the coach portal's panels carry) or `inset` (16px, the subtle hairline on a tint of the quiet surface with its own 16px padding, for an agreement or aside set inside a larger frame). A pill of state is a `Badge`, in one of six tones: `brand-secondary` on the secondary-brand surface, `secondary`, the borderless neutral pill on the `secondary` surface in `secondary-foreground` ink, the outlined `muted` one, `pending` and `success` on their status surfaces, and `count` for a number inside a tab or a `Select` option; a page run is the `Pagination` parts; a status filter that owns its panels is the segmented `Tabs` subpath, while a row of deselectable chips stays `FilterChipGroup`; an appointment is the `AppointmentCard` or, in a dashboard panel, the `DashboardAppointmentRow`, and its row actions are `RowActionButton` and `RowActionLink`, the compact icon-and-label controls; the card can follow its time with a short secondary label, such as the kind of appointment and who made the latest move, and name the author of a quoted note in medium upright text before the note. Joining an appointment from its row is a `JoinLink`: the video glyph and its label as an outline action that fills with `primary` while it is emphasised, at the small action height, or the extra-small one in a compact row. There is one checkbox look, and one multi-choice chip look (`CheckboxChip`), a pill that reads as a native checkbox to assistive technology while carrying its own 44px touch target; a checkbox with a visible statement is a `CheckboxField`, set `inline` beside a short option or as a `statement` whose box aligns to its first line, framed `inset` when it is an agreement, with its problem named through `aria-describedby`. Choosing among several options is a `Select`, matching `Input`'s field height, corner and border, whose options gather under a `SelectGroup` headed by a `SelectLabel` in muted extra-small text and set apart by a hairline `SelectSeparator`, and choosing one of two described options is a `RadioGroup`. A short list of actions on one item, such as a resource's edit and delete, is a `DropdownMenu`: its `DropdownMenuTrigger` opens a `DropdownMenuContent` on the base surface with the field corner, a hairline and the action shadow, whose `DropdownMenuItem`s carry a muted 16px icon, fill with `primary-soft` in `primary` ink while focused, and take the `destructive` variant, danger-toned, for an action that deletes. A person's initials are an `Avatar` in three sizes on the neutral surface, and the coach's client roster sets them with the `heading` tone, in the heading face on the quiet surface. Choosing one of a few short options inline, such as a measurement system, is a `ChoiceGroup`: a segmented row of `ChoiceOption`s on a tint of the quiet surface, the checked one filled in `primary`, one radio group to assistive technology. A date entry is a `DateField`, a trigger in `Input`'s field frame that spells the chosen day out and opens the `Calendar` in a `Popover`, with month and year selects when the field spans years. Choosing an open time is a `SlotPicker`: the `Calendar` of the days that have open times, every other day disabled, and beside it, or below it under `lg`, the chosen day's heading over its times as pressed-state buttons on the `control` corner, the chosen one on `surface-strong`; the times scroll into view when they appear below the calendar, and an inline "change date" link scrolls back to it on small screens. The caller hands it the days, each day's heading and times already worded, the day key of a calendar date and every label, so the picker words nothing and does no time-zone arithmetic of its own. A run of steps is the `Stepper`: the "Step n of m" count with a status slot beside it, above one bar per step that assistive technology does not read. A field's inline problem is a `FieldError`, the danger-toned line the field names through `aria-describedby`, and its description is a `FieldHint`, the muted line named the same way; a `Label` turns danger-toned while its field is in error, a group of fields is headed by a `Legend` in the label ink, a block of fields that is not a fieldset is titled by a `FieldCaption` in the same caption and ink, and a quieter qualifier beside a name, such as a unit or "(optional)", is a `LabelSuffix`. A labelled entry is a `FieldLayout`: the `Label` with its `LabelSuffix`es, wrapped on the text baseline or, for a plain label, set inline, the `FieldHint` before the control or after it when the hint reads the entered value back, the control, and its `FieldError`, the hint and the problem named through the control's `aria-describedby`. A phone number is a `PhoneLink`: a `tel:` link in the surrounding ink that underlines on hover, or the absent-value dash when there is no number. A payment card's brand is a `CardBrandMark`: a 48×32 tile on the base surface with a subtle hairline holding the Visa wordmark, the Mastercard circles or, for any other brand, a neutral card outline in secondary ink, with the brand colours literal inside the drawing; assistive technology skips it, since the brand is written beside it. A dead end inside a portal page, such as a list that could not be read, is a `DeadEndPanel`: the dead-end icon, heading and description centred in a portal panel that announces itself as an alert, with one way on, such as a retry, below the description when there is one. A task's progress is a `Progress` bar: filled to its value, or, when it cannot tell how far it has got, a one-third segment moving across it that holds still at full width and half opacity under reduced motion. Choosing a file is a `FileDropzone`, a dashed field on the quiet surface that takes a dropped or picked file and fills with `primary-soft` while one is held over it, or a `FilePickerButton`, the outline extra-small button that picks one; both are labels around a visually hidden file input, named by their visible text, that draw the shared focus ring. Confirming an action is a `ConfirmDialog`, the one confirmation role, over the `overlay-modal` scrim, taking its `destructive` tone when the confirmed action deletes something; a dialog that is not a confirmation is a `Dialog` with `DialogContent`: `compact` is the ConfirmDialog frame, and `wide` has a fixed header, a scrolling body and a footer set off by a rule, whose actions sit with its content or at its end; a form opened over a page, such as adding measurements, is a `ResponsiveSheetDialog`: a centred dialog on wider screens and a `BottomSheet` below 768px, whose title and description are visually hidden so the form sets its own visible header with `SheetDialogHeader`, scrolls its fields in `SheetDialogBody` and lines up its actions in `SheetDialogActions`, the first action last: below the others on phones, to their right on wider screens, and while its work must not be interrupted, such as a file still uploading, its dismissal is `locked`: Escape, a press outside and dragging the sheet down do nothing and no close control is offered; a photo opened full screen is a `Lightbox`: the dialog frame in its `screen` placement, filling the viewport inside the device's safe-area insets with no border, corner or shadow and no corner close, on the inverted surface, headed by the photo's name with its place in the set and a description line beside one Close button, the photo fitted and moving through the set with previous and next icon buttons, the arrow keys and a horizontal swipe, a double tap or double click toggling a 2× zoom while native pinch-zoom stays on, through the `ZoomableImage` any image viewer shares; a file opened for reading is a `ViewerDialog`: full screen inside the safe-area insets below `lg` and, from `lg`, a centred panel at most 90dvh or 56rem tall and `6xl` wide with the card corner, a border and the raised dialog shadow, without a corner close, its content rendering its own title row and close control; a labelled value in a portal widget, such as a profile or subscription fact, is a `Reading`: the tracked uppercase label above a medium value, as a term and definition inside a description list or as two paragraphs, with a large value rung that carries its unit, a label slot for an adornment after the label and a detail line under the value, extra-small in secondary ink, for a qualifier such as a refund's reason; tabular data is the `Table` parts, and a column that sorts takes `sort` on its `TableHead`, which renders `aria-sort` and a sort button; disclosure sections are the `Accordion` parts, whose trigger renders its own heading, an h3 by default and an h2 when it titles a widget, where its `title` layout centres the chevron on the title line and drops the section layout's vertical padding; secondary detail at the bottom of a page, such as a client's assessment call, is a `CollapsiblePortalWidget`: the portal widget panel whose h2 title is an `Accordion` trigger, collapsed by default; a success mark sits on the `success-surface` token. A short explanation behind an icon, such as the screening warning beside a panel title or the meaning of a term after its label, is an `IconHint`: a round 24px button named for its hint that opens a `Popover` on hover, on a tap and on keyboard focus, stays open through a click, closes when a second tap, Escape, blur or the pointer leaving ends it, describes the button while open, takes the `danger` tone for a warning and the `neutral` one otherwise, and sets its content on the `primary` fill in extra-small `primary-foreground` text. A save outcome is a toast: one `Toaster` in the document layout, so a toast raised as one page hands over to the next survives the navigation, and `toast()` the one way a component reports one. Controls inside `data-surface="inverted"` — the hero, and the header while it is transparent over it — take the lighter focus ring. Weights are regular, medium and semibold, with light kept for the hero's lead copy over video and bold for prices and tracked uppercase `micro` labels. The product targets WCAG AA, keyboard operability everywhere, and layout-stable reduced-motion fallbacks.

Both portals share one `PortalShell`. On large screens a fixed sidebar carries the coach's brand block or, in the client portal, her name block, an icon medallion beside her full name that links to her profile page, above the navigation. The medallion is its own role rather than an `Avatar`: a round `User` glyph in `primary` ink on `primary-soft`, 40px in the sidebar and the More sheet and 36px in the top bar, standing in until a client picture exists. Below `lg` a 64px top bar, named for its portal, carries the same block; the coach's navigation opens in a drawer, while the client has a bottom tab bar of at most four tabs plus More, whose `BottomSheet` holds her name block, the links the bar has no room for and Sign out; following her name block or a link from the sheet closes it. Her Profile is both a sidebar link and a tab beside Dashboard. A navigation link can be marked as holding something new: the shell draws the attention dot, an 8px circle in `status-pending`, at the end of the sidebar link, between the label and the chevron in the More sheet, and on the corner of a tab's icon ringed in `surface-base`, and assistive technology hears the link's label followed by "(new)"; the More tab carries the same mark while any link in its sheet is marked. The top bar pads its top, and the tab bar and the sheet their bottom, by the device's safe-area insets, so the layout keeps clear of the notch and the home indicator. The skip link is the one control that must paint above every fixed piece of page chrome, the portal sidebar and top bar and the public header included, while a modal dialog or sheet keeps it from taking focus at all, and its layer is set once in the shared skip-link rule. The client portal installs as its own app: its name and short name are "Evoa", its theme colour is `surface-base`, so the title bar reads as part of the shell, and its background colour is `surface-page`; the PWA surface definition holds them as those two tokens' hex values.
