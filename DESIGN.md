# Design

## Identity

Evoa has a warm, premium, and modern visual identity. Soft off-white surfaces, restrained borders and shadows, and generous whitespace keep the product calm and approachable. Magenta is the primary brand and action color; teal is the grounded counterpoint for secondary actions and recovery-oriented content. Elegant serif headings in `Playfair Display` add personality, while `DM Sans` keeps the interface practical and easy to scan.

The design balances human coaching with professional competence. Photography and small product-UI compositions may add warmth and context, but the interface itself stays clean, focused, and consistent across the public site, client portal, and coach portal. The landing page carries more visual impact and motion than the portals, which favor calm, predictable structure for use mid-workout and while managing detailed plans.

## Audience

Women seeking personalized strength and nutrition coaching, with or without an active menstrual cycle. The warm palette, editorial headings, supportive copy hierarchy, and body-aware domain colors for training day types, cycle phases, and flow intensity make the experience feel personal without stereotypically feminine decoration.

## Source of Truth

The design system lives in code. Read it there rather than expecting a catalogue here:

- Production tokens: `packages/ui/src/styles.css`. Production components and variants: `packages/ui` has no root barrel; it publishes eleven concern subpaths instead (`appointments`, `calendar`, `filters`, `layout`, `lib`, `motion`, `overlays`, `portal`, `primitives`, `tabs`, `toast`), each imported on its own; `portal` holds the page chrome both portals share (page header, widget frame, widget link, settings frame, empty state).
- The prototype in `designs/react-reference-app` mirrors the same visual roles and may carry tokens and compositions production does not have yet. Its `DESIGN.md` and `brand-voice.md` describe it.

Every color, spacing, radius, shadow, and typography value is a semantic token; raw values appear only in non-reusable layout mechanics. A token name means the same value in production and in the prototype. Radii, control sizes and letter-spacing are named by role (`rounded-card`, `rounded-field`, `rounded-control`, `size-control-lg`, `tracking-label`); the framework's size-named steps are never overridden, and a role that would equal one (0.025em tracking is `tracking-wide`) is not added. Every role name is registered with `cn`'s class merger, so a later class of the same kind replaces it. Every text action is a `Button` or `buttonVariants`: one base whose `corner` is the 8px `field` corner or the 14px `control` one, sizes that set a height, a padding that narrows beside a leading or trailing icon (`md-wide` keeps its padding), and a text size (`xl` is the 60px checkout action; round icon sizes set only a size), widths that set only a width, one raised elevation, one disabled look and the one focus rule. The interaction colour is `primary`: the brand magenta on the public site and inside the client portal, near-black inside the coach portal, set by the document's `data-portal` scope so one `primary` button, chip, tab or link reads correctly in every place. Two fills stay outside that scope: `ink`, near-black turning brand on hover, and `on-brand`, the base surface with brand text for an action on a brand-coloured banner. Every card is a `Card` or `cardVariants`: `card` (16px, faint border, card shadow), `quiet` (flat, on the quiet surface), `panel` (24px, for large frames) or `portal-panel` (24px, the faint hairline the coach portal's panels carry). A pill of state is a `Badge`, in one of five tones: `brand-secondary` on the secondary-brand surface, the outlined `muted` one, `pending` and `success` on their status surfaces, and `count` for a number inside a tab or a `Select` option; a page run is the `Pagination` parts; a status filter that owns its panels is the segmented `Tabs` subpath, while a row of deselectable chips stays `FilterChipGroup`; an appointment is the `AppointmentCard` or, in a dashboard panel, the `DashboardAppointmentRow`. There is one checkbox look, and one multi-choice chip look (`CheckboxChip`), a pill that reads as a native checkbox to assistive technology while carrying its own 44px touch target. Choosing among several options is a `Select`, matching `Input`'s field height, corner and border, and choosing one of two described options is a `RadioGroup`. Confirming an action is a `ConfirmDialog`, the one confirmation role, over the `overlay-modal` scrim; a success mark sits on the `success-surface` token. A save outcome is a toast: one `Toaster` per shell, and `toast()` the one way a component reports one. Controls inside `data-surface="inverted"` — the hero, and the header while it is transparent over it — take the lighter focus ring. Weights are regular, medium and semibold, with light kept for the hero's lead copy over video and bold for prices and tracked uppercase `micro` labels. The product targets WCAG AA, keyboard operability everywhere, and layout-stable reduced-motion fallbacks.
