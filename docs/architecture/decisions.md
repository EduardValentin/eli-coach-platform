## Cohesion groups

The groups themselves are recorded in `model/boundaries.json` and kept current with `model
add-group` and `model remove-group`; read them with `query groups`. This section holds, per
group, one paragraph on why it exists: the kind of change it absorbs and why its components
belong together.

**vertical-feature-slice.** One user-visible capability delivered end to end is the repository's
ticket-cutting unit: the domain rule and its port in a component's `domain/`, its adapters and
`config.server.ts`, a design-system piece in C5, a shared concern when the slice needs one (most
often C47 email or C48 http), the bounded context's published entry and assembly rows when the
slice crosses components, the surfaces that register them, the container in C14, the route
registry in C15, the migration journal under `apps/platform/db`, and C16 when its tests need a
shared fixture. A slice usually but not always touches the composition root and the journal, so
a member's absence does not on its own make a change uncontained. A slice touches more than one
component when its component consumes another's facade or published entry, or its tables
(foreign keys). Confirmed by the owner.

**waiting-list-mode.** How waiting-list mode is stored, read and switched spans the
`WAITLIST_MODE` flag's persistence and override decorator in C28, the operations feature-flags
facade in C30, the waitlist (C8), booking-window (C17) and sales-window (C18) consumers, each
through its own waitlist-mode port over that facade, the container in C14 that hands the facade
to all of them, and the seed under C15 and `apps/platform/db` that inserts it. The spread across
C8, C14 and C15 is owner-accepted; C17, C18, C28 and C30 are added from the code, unconfirmed.

**coach-scheduling.** The coach's own time and room change as one concern: slot policy,
reservation and overlap rules and the coach's window and meeting room in C23, the scheduling
facades in C33 and their assembly in C39, the two appointment kinds that reserve that time
(assessment calls in C17, check-ins in C22), the container in C14 that hands the scheduling
facades on, and their tables and constraints in the migration journal. Proposed from change
history, unconfirmed.

**adapter-facing-contracts.** A change to the email (C47), bot-detection (C54), management-auth
(C7) or payments (C18) contract lands on the contract and adapter and, at once, on every
component and platform module that sends, verifies or handles through it, so those components
move together. Proposed from change history, unconfirmed.

**design-system.** A token, primitive or shared look (focus, disabled state, corner, typography,
field frame, dialog frame) changes once in C5 and is taken up by every consumer: the components
with a browser half, the three surfaces and the app root. Proposed from change history,
unconfirmed.

**coach-portal.** A coach-facing block is delivered as a vertical slice through the coach's side:
a listing or sales use case and its port method in the component's `domain/`, a portal primitive
in C5, the coach UI half of a component, and the page or navigation in C13 that composes it.
Owner-accepted as a vertical slice.

**operations-bounded-context.** The operator's switches: the persisted feature flags, their
browser override in LOCAL and TEST, and the feature-flags facade every waiting-list-mode reader
goes through (C28, its published facade C30 and its assembly C36). Owner-approved with the
bounded-context layout.

**legal-bounded-context.** The legal documents and their versions: the terms, the privacy policy
and the store marketing consent, the hashes the store and the waitlist record against, and the
terms facade sales reads (C29, C31, C37). Owner-approved with the bounded-context layout.

**access-bounded-context.** Accounts, sessions and portal access: Clerk, the account-resolution
middleware, the resolved session every surface and component reads, the account invitations the
clients side allows through the account-invitations facade, and the portal paths (C9, C32, C38).
Owner-approved with the bounded-context layout.

**scheduling-bounded-context.** The coach's time and the assessment call: her window, room and
reserved time, the booking, and the client-calls, ended-calls, coach-time, meeting-room and
open-slots facades the clients and sales bounded contexts read (C17, C23, C33, C39).
Owner-approved with the bounded-context layout.

**clients-bounded-context.** Who a client is and what she receives: the client row, her
invitation and journey, her profile and onboarding, her resources, the bell and her check-ins,
with the admissions, client-lookup, client-profile and coaching-periods facades sales reads
(C20, C21, C22, C24, C27, C34, C40). Owner-approved with the bounded-context layout.

**programs-bounded-context.** The coach's exercise and nutrition libraries, and the program and
meal plans that will read them (C25, C26, C41). Owner-approved with the bounded-context layout.

**sales-bounded-context.** What is sold and how: the store, the waitlist and its reduced pricing,
and coaching sales from the payment link to the subscription's lifecycle (C7, C8, C18, C35,
C42). Owner-approved with the bounded-context layout.
